## VERDICT: NOT DECIDED
  This compares the two populations as built: body, controller topology and mutation operator all differ between them. It does not test the variability mechanism in isolation.
  RBT-113's D2 disclaimers apply: "this is the response to imposed selection in this design; it is not a measurement of natural selection in the ecology (paper 5 §2–§4)"; "realised h2 of this design only; not comparable with paper 5's parent–offspring r".
  The primary quantity is in raw net-yield units, the currency both faunas are scored in: a larger raw response can come from more phenotypic variation, more heritability or both, or from the down line's room to lose yield by working harder, which differs between the bodies; the sigma0-unit line beside it is the per-unit-of-variation reading and is not scored.
  Founder sigma0 here: holistic 0.2187, designed 0.7545. Reason (b)'s premise, that the holistic population is the more variable, does not hold on this trait; a designed win is not by itself evidence against the mechanism.
