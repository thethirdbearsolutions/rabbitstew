# RBT-28: a shared drive motif, not a shared gait — and one of the four "inert noses" is not a nose

Five holistic champions plus a Pioneer contrast, autopsied with `scripts/forage_lab.py` at 12 fresh
draws each, every mode read against **its own trajectory-preserving null** rather than a point-robot
floor (RBT-39, the coordinator's condition on this ticket). Pre-registration posted on the issue at
04:56 UTC before any champion bout ran, and unchanged since.

Branch `claude/rbt-lowest-unclaimed-ticket-ems6yl`. Raw output `runs/RBT-28/lab.txt` and
`runs/RBT-28/pioneer_contrast.txt`; champions regenerated byte-identically by `runs/RBT-28/extract.sh`.
No champion, config or seed was changed.

## The answer in one paragraph

**They are not the same gait, and they are not four unrelated solutions either.** The *drive* is the
same kind of thing in four of five — a single effector whose lesion costs most of the yield — and **no
champion in the set drives through an oscillator at all** (`no_osc` is exactly +0.00 on all five). But
what surrounds that drive splits three ways: the global brain is load-bearing in two champions
(+2.25 and +1.25 items), irrelevant in one (+0.00), and **actively harmful in two** (−1.00, −1.25, i.e.
they eat more with it silenced). By the rule I pre-registered — same top-lesion kind *and* one drive
row shape *and* a whole-subsystem profile agreeing in sign — **the answer is no: rule 3 fails.** The
honest summary is a shared drive motif inside four different controllers, and the fifth champion has
no load-bearing unit at all.

## 1. Q1: are these the same gait?

**Shared, on four of five:** the most costly single-unit lesion is an **effector**, and the drive runs
through it rather than through an oscillator or a bias into a local neuron. `no_osc` costs exactly
nothing on every champion — **no oscillator is load-bearing anywhere in this family's mowers**, which
is worth recording because the oscillator is the obvious way to build a gait and none of them used it.

**Not shared:** the whole-subsystem profile, which splits three ways.

| champion | `no_global` | `no_local` | `no_env` | reading |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RBT-13 g390 | **+2.25** | +0.42 | +0.25 | the global brain *is* the drive |
| RBT-16 g590 | **+1.25** | +0.75 | +0.33 | global brain load-bearing |
| RBT-17 g590 | +0.00 | +0.00 | **−0.83** | nothing load-bearing; its sensorium hurts it |
| RBT-22 g590 | **−1.00** | −0.92 | **−0.92** | every subsystem is a handicap |
| baseline-801 g300 | **−1.25** | **−1.67** | **−0.75** | every subsystem is a handicap |

Two of five eat *more* with their global brain silenced, and two of five depend on it entirely. That
is not one attractor.

**The Pioneer is the control that makes the shared part meaningful.** Its top lesion is a global
`tanh` neuron, not an effector, and its whole-subsystem profile is different again. So "an effector
drive, no oscillator" is not a property of any robot in this simulator — it is what the *evolved*
bodies converged on and the designed one did not.

**The caveat I pre-registered, honoured.** These arms are 13 to 20 reproduction events deep
(`depth ≈ 2 × seasons / max_age`, `max_age = 60`, verified in all five configs). At that depth
"a single attractor the search found" and "structure the founders already shared, never diverged
from" are both live, **and this autopsy cannot separate them.** The convergence is real; its cause is
not established here. Separating them needs the founders, not the champions.

## 2. Q2: the noses — and a correction to the ticket's premise

The ticket says four arms produced "a holistic best that carries a smell sensor and gets nothing from
it". That is right for three of them and **wrong for the fourth**, in a way that matters:

- **RBT-13, RBT-16 and RBT-17 carry food sensors with zero outgoing links.** Blanking them is a
  no-op by construction, not an empirical finding. `no_smell +0.00` on those three is arithmetic.
- **RBT-22's g590 carries no food or agent sensor at all** — only joint-angle sensors. The ticket
  listed it as "this arm's nosed champion ... if the arm produces one", and the arm did not: RBT-22's
  own report already said 0 of 60 saved holistic bests carry a nose. **That row of the ticket's table
  is empty.**
- **Only `baseline-801` g300 has a nose wired to anything** (unit 4 → two local units, weights −0.27
  and +0.54). It is the one place in this set where `no_smell` could have moved.

**So "inert nose" understates it. In four of five the wiring was never there.** That is the same
finding as RBT-22's seven expressed-but-unlinked noses and RBT-62's magnitude gap, from a third angle:
the search does not propose the link, so there is nothing for selection to act on.

### My Q2 prediction was partly unfalsifiable, and I did not notice when I wrote it

I pre-registered "`no_smell` costs < 25% on all five, confidence 0.85". It holds — but on four of five
champions it **had** to hold, because there is no link to cut. The prediction was only ever testable on
`baseline-801` g300. Scoring it as a passed prediction would be dressing up a tautology.

### The pre-registered exception FAILED, and it kills a standing claim

I predicted (confidence 0.7) that `baseline-801` g300 would lose ~50% of its yield with the nose
blanked alone and ~0% with everything blanked — the "the food input's job is to cancel a harmful
joint-angle input" mechanism in `docs/foraging-world.md`.

**At 12 draws it loses 0.08 items of 1.33, which is 6%.** Published: 1.75 → 0.88, a 50% loss on eight
seeds. The *other* half of that description survives and strengthens: blanking every sensor raises
yield (1.33 → 2.08, `no_env` −0.75), so its sensorium does hurt it on net.

**This is the family's one known nose-with-a-non-smelling-job, and the "half the yield" figure does
not survive.** It is an eight-seed reading moving downward at higher n, which is the RBT-38 pattern
exactly — ten of fifteen died, and every one that moved, moved down. `docs/foraging-world.md` needs
correcting and I have done it beside the original wording rather than silently.

## 3. Q3: the series' headline mower, re-read

| claim | published (8 seeds) | 12 draws | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| RBT-13 g390 items | 3.12 | **2.42 ± 0.56** | inside the 95% interval [1.32, 3.52] — **survives my stated rule**, but the point estimate is 22% lower |
| RBT-13 g390 work | 1.2 kJ | **1.25 kJ** | holds |
| items per kJ over the Pioneer | "roughly thirty times" | **1.94 vs 0.044 = 44×** | **survives, and is an understatement** |

I gave the item count 0.45 and the energy gap 0.8. The item count came in lower as expected; the
energy gap came in better than expected. **But my rule for the item count was too lenient and I want
that on the record:** "inside its own 95% interval of 3.12" is a test almost any 12-draw re-read of a
noisy 8-seed figure would pass, because the interval is ±1.1 items wide. The informative statement is
that the point estimate fell 22%, in the same direction as every other re-read this programme has
done. A stricter rule — the published figure inside the *new* interval *and* the new point estimate
within 10% — would have failed, and I should have pre-registered that instead.

The **energy** claim is the durable one, for the reason I predicted: a ratio of actuator work is far
less noisy than an item count. The evolved lump does 44× the Pioneer's foraging per joule.

**One thing the title has to move on.** RBT-13 g390 is recorded as "the strongest mower of the
series". On 12 draws in its own world it eats 2.42 items; **RBT-16's g590 eats 4.08** in its own. The
worlds differ (24 items against 12), so this is not a like-for-like defeat and I am not claiming one —
but "strongest mower" was never measured across arms and should not be stated as if it had been.

## 4. Q4: the RBT-39 question, on five more champions and a Pioneer

**Not one of the six beats what its own gait meets by accident.** Paired t over 12 draws: −0.06,
−0.75, −1.15, +0.22, −1.26, and −1.80 for the Pioneer, against a bar of 2.5. Confidence 0.9, held.

With RBT-39's ten, that is **sixteen champions measured against their own gaits and no compass
anywhere.** Four of the six here read *below* their own null (though none significantly), which is
what a robot that re-covers eaten ground looks like.

Every swept corridor is far wider than the point-robot rate's assumed 0.70 m — 1.08 m to 2.87 m —
which is why the floor was never the right comparison for any of them.

## 6. Method, and its limits

Bouts: each champion alone in its own arm's world from its own `config.json`, `random_start=True`,
`spawn_layout(1, cfg, seed)`, `set_food_seed(seed)`, seeds 8000+, 12 draws, 120 world-dealt null
layouts per bout. Modes: intact, `no_env`, `no_smell`, `no_osc`, `no_global`, `no_local`, and every
linked unit lesioned alone. Items eaten carries a standard error over draws, which is the whole point
of re-reading eight-seed figures.

Two corrections to the inherited `forage_lab.py` (it existed on `feature/RBT-28` at `62726ed`, 92
commits behind and untested; brought forward rather than rewritten):

- its header printed the point-robot floor as the thing a compass beats. Replaced by each mode's own
  trajectory null, with the closed form still printed and labelled reference-only.
- it sampled the centre-of-mass path **every tenth control tick**, which understates path and so
  overstates items per metre by up to 1.17×. Now every tick, pinned by a test.

Limits, stated: 12 draws is enough to kill a 50% eight-seed claim and not enough to establish a small
one; the depth caveat in §1 is the real limit on the convergence finding; `no_global`/`no_local` are
whole-subsystem blanks and a champion whose drive is one unit inside a subsystem will show the same
profile as one whose drive is distributed across it; and the Pioneer contrast is one Pioneer from one
arm, chosen because it shares RBT-13's world and season, not sampled.

## 7. What the adversary changed (RBT-67 delegate, 06:03 UTC) — three attacks landed

Their post is on the ticket; PR #27, probes `runs/RBT-28/adversary_founders.py` and `adversary_n64.py`.
They reproduced `lab.txt` to the last digit first. **Three of their attacks land, one of my findings is
strengthened by them, and one thing I asserted was answerable all along and I did not check.**

### The founders were reachable from the seed, and I said they were not

§1 above says *"separating them needs the founders"* and that this autopsy cannot do it. **Wrong.**
Every one of the five runs has `seed = 801`, and `initial_population(HOLISTIC, cfg, default_rng(801))`
regenerates all sixty founders; founder 49 is byte-identical to every run's saved
`best_gen0000.json` (`h0-49`). I verified this independently before accepting it.

**I asserted data was unavailable without checking whether it was regenerable** — the same error I had
made on RBT-65 an hour earlier and had explicitly flagged in myself. A seed is an artifact.

**And it reframes §1's headline.** The five arms are **five worlds run on one founding population**, so
"four of five arms converged" is not four independent searches arriving at the same structure. The base
rate over the sixty founders: **29 of 60 already carry a link-driven effector and no linked
oscillator**, and P(≥ 4 of 5 sharing it) = **0.17**. So the shared *effector drive* is suggestive, not
established. §1's reading stands as a description and must not be cited as evidence of an attractor.

### "No oscillator" is stronger than I claimed, by a better route

I read it off the five champions. They read it off the search: **13 of 60 founders carry a linked
oscillator, the season-0 best is one of them, and 1 of 295 saved bests after season 0 carries one.**
Every run's best lost the oscillator link between seasons 0 and 10 and, bar one (RBT-16 g430), never
regained it. That is not the founders' distribution — it is the search **discarding** oscillator drive in
five worlds, which is a regularity over evolutionary time rather than a property of five champions.
Their statement replaces mine.

### The 6% is not a number either, and my "net handicap" reading is withdrawn

At **64 paired seeds** on `baseline-801` g300:

| lesion | intact − lesion | paired t | zeros | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `no_smell` | **−0.047 ± 0.166** | −0.28 | 27/64 | not an effect |
| `no_env` | **−0.281 ± 0.214** | −1.31 | 23/64 | **not an effect** |

The 50% claim is as dead as §2 says, but "6%" was a 12-draw artifact and the honest figure is
**0.00 ± 0.17**. Worse for me: §1's table and §2 both read `no_env` as showing *"every subsystem is a
handicap"* for this champion. **That does not survive and is withdrawn** — it was a twelve-draw figure
moving toward zero at 64, which is the exact pattern I had been applying to everyone else's numbers.
`docs/foraging-world.md` now carries both corrections.

### Q3 fails at adequate n, and my rule was worse than lenient

RBT-13 g390 at **64 draws: 2.031 ± 0.223 items, interval [1.59, 2.47]. The published 3.12 is outside
it.** My 12 draws were the first twelve of the same seed series and ran high. The per-draw sd is 1.78
items, so detecting a 22% drop at |t| ≥ 2.5 needs about **99 draws**.

**So twelve draws could not have tested Q3 at all: the design could not fail.** "Too lenient" was the
wrong self-criticism — the rule was untestable at the n I chose, which is worse, because it looks like a
measurement. The honest statement, which replaces §3's: **the series' headline item count is
2.03 ± 0.22 at 64 draws, 35% below the published 3.12, which lies outside the interval.**

The energy claim survives at the same n: **1.66 items per kJ against the Pioneer's 0.044 — 38×**, where
I reported 44× at twelve and "thirty times" was published. Work is a ratio of near-constant quantities
(se 0.007 kJ), which is why it was the durable half.

### The tooling change this forced

`scripts/forage_lab.py` now prints, after every table, the effect size its own n can resolve at
|t| ≥ 2.5 and how many draws a 25% effect would need — and says plainly when the n **cannot test** one.
A test pins it. That is the check that would have stopped §3 from being written, and it is the same
lesson as RBT-39's retired 7%: a number standing in for a measurement nobody took.

### What they could not break

`no_global` +1.828 ± 0.208 at t = +8.77 on RBT-13 g390 (the global brain is the drive at any n);
RBT-16 g590's 4.078 ± 0.370 at 64, identical to twelve; and the premise correction in §2, which is
arithmetic on committed genotypes.
