# RBT-71 Deliverable B: citation verification log (what was checked, where, and the gap probes; 2026-09-14, ~10:52-11:05 UTC)

VERIFIED against the paper's own record/abstract:
- Mertan, A. & Cheney, N. "Evolutionary Brain-Body Co-Optimization Consistently Fails to Select for Morphological Potential." arXiv:2508.17464; accepted, Artificial Life (journal); extended from ALIFE 2025. 1,305,840 voxel-based soft robots exhaustively trained. Finding: co-optimisation gets trapped near suboptimal morphologies, undervalues newly mutated bodies, discards promising designs; yet co-optimisation reaches morphology-controller pairs fixed-morphology optimisation cannot ("goal-switching"). [arxiv.org/abs/2508.17464]
- Cheney, N., Bongard, J., SunSpiral, V. & Lipson, H. "On the Difficulty of Co-Optimizing Morphology and Control in Evolved Virtual Creatures." Proc. ALIFE 2016 (15th Int. Conf. Synthesis and Simulation of Living Systems), Cancun; MIT Press, pp. 226-233. Abstract: field stagnant in complexification; premature convergence of morphology relative to controllers, reproduced and explored; framed via embodied cognition. NOTE: morphological innovation protection is NOT this paper's proposal; it is the 2018 follow-up (below). The ticket's attribution needs correcting. [direct.mit.edu/isal/proceedings/alif2016/28/226/99427]
- Miconi, T. "The Evolution of Foraging in an Open-Ended Simulation Environment." EPIA 2011 (15th Portuguese Conf. on AI), Lisbon; Springer LNCS, doi 10.1007/978-3-642-24769-9_10. Multi-agent ant-foraging-inspired world; evolves rule-list brains "without any explicit fitness function"; introduces "step evolution". Controller-only (no morphology). [link.springer.com]
- Chaumont, N. & Adami, C. "Evolution of sustained foraging in three-dimensional environments with physics." Genetic Programming and Evolvable Machines (2016), doi 10.1007/s10710-016-9270-z; arXiv:1112.5116 (2011). Co-evolves morphology AND controller of articulated 3D creatures for foraging in physics -- BUT with an explicit, staged fitness function ("evaluating each organism on multiple food sources ... a particular fitness function ... progressively altered"). No hand-designed body in the abstract. => closest related work on the co-evolved side; NOT the fitness-free configuration.
- Spector, L., Klein, J. & Feinstein, M. "Division Blocks and the Open-Ended Evolution of Development, Form, and Behavior." GECCO 2007, London; ACM, pp. 316-323, doi 10.1145/1276958.1277019. Physically simulated blocks, RNN controllers, "evolve, along with the blocks, by natural selection"; energy approximately conserved, from a simulated sun via photosynthesis.
- Soros, L. B. & Stanley, K. O. "Identifying Necessary Conditions for Open-Ended Evolution through the Artificial Life World of Chromaria." ALIFE 14 (2014), MIT Press. Four conditions incl. a minimal criterion for reproduction.
- Yaeger, L. S. "Computational Genetics, Physiology, Metabolism, Neural Systems, Learning, Vision, and Behavior or PolyWorld: Life in a New Context." Artificial Life III (Langton ed.), Addison-Wesley 1994, pp. 263-298. Metabolism, Hebbian learning, vision, ecology with speciation.
- Channon, A. "Passing the ALife Test: Activity Statistics Classify Evolution in Geb as Unbounded." ECAL 2001, Prague; Springer LNCS 2159, doi 10.1007/3-540-44811-X_45. (Also Channon 2006, component-normalised, "Improving and still passing...".)

PENDING: Ray 1991 (Tierra); Avida (Adami & Brown 1994; Ofria & Wilke 2004); Sims 1994; Cheney et al. 2018 (MIP, J. R. Soc. Interface?); Miconi & Channon Evosphere; prior-art probes for a designed body inside a fitness-free ecology.

VERIFIED (second batch):
- Cheney, N., Bongard, J., SunSpiral, V. & Lipson, H. "Scalable co-optimization of morphology and control in embodied machines." J. R. Soc. Interface 15(143): 20170937, 2018, doi 10.1098/rsif.2017.0937. THIS is where 'morphological innovation protection' (temporarily reducing selection pressure on recently morphologically changed individuals) is demonstrated. Ticket RBT-71/RBT-73 credit it to the 2016 paper: correct to 2018.
- Ray, T. S. "An approach to the synthesis of life." Artificial Life II (Langton, Taylor, Farmer, Rasmussen eds), SFI Studies in the Sciences of Complexity, Addison-Wesley 1991, pp. 371-408. Tierra: self-replicating machine code in memory, no fitness function.
- Adami, C. & Brown, C. T. "Evolutionary learning in the 2D artificial life system 'Avida'." Artificial Life IV, MIT Press 1994, pp. 377-381. Ofria, C. & Wilke, C. O. "Avida: a software platform for research in computational evolutionary biology." Artificial Life 10(2): 191-229, 2004, doi 10.1162/106454604773563612.
- Sims, K. "Evolving virtual creatures." SIGGRAPH '94, Computer Graphics Annual Conf. Series, ACM, pp. 15-22 (explicit fitness functions: swimming, walking, jumping, following). Sims, K. "Evolving 3D morphology and behavior by competition." Artificial Life IV, MIT Press 1994, pp. 28-39; journal version Artificial Life 1(4): 353-372, 1994 (one-on-one contests for a common resource; winners get higher relative fitness -- a ranked competitive score, which is exactly the 2005 proposal's instrument).
- Miconi & Channon, Evosphere: reimplementation of Sims's creatures on a "microplanet" with damage/fighting, "free natural selection without defining a fitness function", co-evolved morphology + embedded NN controllers. => fitness-free + co-evolved morphology + 3D physics, i.e. the nearest prior configuration on the evolved side; NO designed body. Exact refs pending (Miconi & Channon 2005 CEC "A virtual creatures model..."; 2006 ALIFE X "An improved system..."; Miconi 2008 CEC "Evosphere: evolutionary dynamics in a population of fighting virtual creatures"; Miconi 2008 ALIFE XI "In silicon no one can hear you scream").

VERIFIED (third batch):

- Miconi, T. "Evosphere: evolutionary dynamics in a population of fighting virtual creatures." IEEE CEC 2008 (ieeexplore 4631212). 3D creatures fight and "evolve freely on the surface of a microplanet"; onset of natural selection shown. - (Ecological Complexity 22: 139-151, 2015, "How morphology of artificial organisms influences their evolution": morphology-dependent foraging and reproduction, behaviour not prescribed; competition of phenotypes; authors not captured -- cite only if needed, else omit.)
- Non-peer-reviewed: avivshaked/cambrian (GitHub): Unity ecosystem, bodies+brains co-evolved, "There is no fitness function", energy-based death; NO hand-designed baseline, no quantitative comparison. Confirms the configuration is in the air as software, not in the literature as a measured comparison. Mention at most in a footnote-level clause; probably omit.
