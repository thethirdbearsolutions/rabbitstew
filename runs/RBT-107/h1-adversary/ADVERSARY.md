### J5. "H1's absence argues against an F2-sized effect persisting at T + 800" is stronger than the data. MUST-FIX

**Where:** REPORT.md, "What the absent verdict excludes", bullet 3. "What this does and does not say" leans on the same
reading.

**The claim rests on power alone:** 0.83 (DES) and 0.72 (PAIR) at F2's sizes, on a Gaussian model of the realised null.
The data themselves do not reject F2's sizes. `power_check.py` (→ `power_check.txt`) shows three things.

**1. F2's size lies inside the 95% t interval of every scored component on the common set.**

| component | F2 | observed mean [95% CI] | registered Yuen p that the effect is weaker than F2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| DES A_SB | −0.22 | −0.106 [−0.266, +0.054] | 0.072 |
| DES A_SN | −0.22 | −0.097 [−0.265, +0.071] | 0.162 |
| PAIR P | +0.27 | +0.152 [−0.059, +0.362] | 0.035 |
| PAIR P_N | +0.27 | +0.174 [−0.020, +0.368] | 0.240 |

An F2-sized DES decline is not rejected on either component. On the same one-sided test, the data are as compatible
with F2's size as with zero.

**2. The model understates the scored components' spread.**
- The observed sd over the realised-null model's sd is 1.24 (DES A_SB), 1.29 (DES A_SN), 1.13 (P) and 1.04 (P_N).
- The shift arm adds between-seed spread that the null (cull20 − base) does not have.
- The faunas' A_SB are uncorrelated (+0.01), so the model's independence assumption is not the problem.

**3. A residual bootstrap of the observed seeds gives lower power.** Method: the four components per seed, centred,
shifted to F2's sizes, and resampled at n = 19 with the registered Yuen, IUT and Holm.
- At F2's sizes: Holm DES 0.52 and Holm PAIR 0.57, against the report's 0.83 and 0.72.
- At half F2: 0.10 and 0.07. Under the null: 0.01 and 0.00.
- A bootstrap at n = 19 slightly understates spread, so these figures if anything flatter the power.

**So:**
- The absence is weak evidence against F2's size: a likelihood ratio of about 0.5 / 0.2 on the bootstrap, not about
  0.8 / 0.2.
- The intervals do not exclude F2's size.
- "Says little about half that size" is right. "Argues against F2-sized" should become something like:

> "At F2's sizes the scored IUT had 0.83 (DES) / 0.72 (PAIR) power on a Gaussian model of the realised null, but only
> about 0.5 on the observed spread; F2's sizes lie inside every component's 95% interval. The absence does not exclude an
> F2-sized effect at T + 800."

**The same fix is needed in "It does not say".** It should add "nor that an F2-sized effect is absent".

**The designer's RBT-107 summary (14:22 UTC) repeats this reading and J6's.** The fix should reach that summary too.

**`power.txt` itself is not wrong** (J9). It is the reading that is too strong.

### J6. "Designed effects larger than about 0.21 income per bout are excluded on A_SB/A_SN" is unsigned and too strong. MUST-FIX

**Where:** REPORT.md, "What this does and does not say" → "It does say", bullet 3.

**Why it is too strong:**
- 0.209 is the two-interval rule's 80% detection resolution on the realised null. It is a power figure, not a
  confidence bound.
- On the decline side, the scored intervals reach **−0.283 (A_SB) and −0.264 (A_SN)**. A designed decline of −0.25 is
  inside both, and so is F2's −0.22 (J5).
- §10's registered exclusion is about a **flat-ground response**, meaning a gain: "no flat-ground response larger than
  X". On that side the data do agree. The upper limits are +0.031 (A_SB) and +0.055 (A_SN), well under 0.21.

**Fix:**
- Sign it, e.g. "a designed gain on flat ground larger than about 0.21 income per bout (the realised resolution) is
  excluded; declines are not".
- Or drop "excluded" and give the resolution with its meaning.
- **SHOULD-FIX, the §10 section:** "there is no flat-ground designed response … larger than about 0.21" is §10's own
  wording. It should carry the same sign and say that the figure is an 80% detection resolution.
