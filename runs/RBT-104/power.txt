q = P(an S8 population's champions carry a paying compass | the hypothesis); each S8 population
then reads FOOD-DEPENDENT with p = q * d.  With 10 usable S8 populations (fewer: see the VOID rule):

| q | p = q d | P(FALSIFIED's count: <= 1 of 10) | P(SUPPORTED's count: >= 5 of 10) | P(no paying carrier in any S8 window) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.2 | 0.16 | 0.493 | 0.014 | 0.1074 |
| 0.3 | 0.25 | 0.253 | 0.074 | 0.0282 |
| 0.5 | 0.41 | 0.040 | 0.393 | 0.0010 |
| 0.7 | 0.57 | 0.003 | 0.788 | 0.0000 |
| 0.9 | 0.74 | 0.000 | 0.975 | 0.0000 |

  With only 7 usable (the VOID floor), P(<= 1 of 7) at q = 0.5 is 0.146 and at q = 0.3 is 0.454.
  Reading: FALSIFIED is a fair test of the hypothesis if it implies q >= ~0.5; at q = 0.2-0.3
  (selection keeps a compass in only a fifth to a third of populations) the null fires with
  substantial probability under the hypothesis, and the report must say FALSIFIED means
  'selection did not keep a paying compass in most populations', not 'in none'.
