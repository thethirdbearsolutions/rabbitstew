## 1. τ per point (B1)

- `REGISTERED_POINTS` gains RBT-129's 18 PAYS cells (`RBT129_POINTS`, in `blocks.PAYS_CELLS` order; the 4 pilot points
  are among them). Each row is what the committed `runs/RBT-129/worlds/<id>.config.json` holds:
  - G points: `smell_contrast` 2.5, `smell_tau` 2.0;
  - L points: `smell_contrast` 0 (no channel), with `smell_tau` 2.0 sitting unused;
  - everywhere: `eat_from root`, `eat_rule surface`, `clear_from root`, `eat_radius` 0.35.

  A test checks all 18 against their committed configs.
- **`assert_registered_channel(cfg, point)`** refuses a channel whose τ differs from **that point's** registered τ. It
  also refuses a channel at a point that registers none (an L point), and any unregistered point (KeyError). W1's check
  is exactly as before.
- **`run_season(..., point)` and `point_season(point)`**: the season function `call_genome` and `screen_draws` take.
  `screen_draws(world=<an RBT-129 point>)` uses that point's season by default. The CLI (`--world`) and `_job` pass the
  point through.
- **`assert_fair_config(raw, point)`**: an RBT-129 point runs only on a config carrying RBT-128's `"fairness": "fair"`,
  as `steps.py`'s S12 requires. W1's block predates `--fair`, and needs no marker.

## 2. Draw pools and the reachability screen (B2)

- **`POOL_KEY[pid] = (116, 64, 129, k)`**, k = 1…18 in `RBT129_POINTS` order. These are registered here, before any
  season runs on them. Each pool has 64 draws plus the 32-draw extension, **built exactly as W1's**
  (`draw_pool`). A test shows that every point's pool is distinct and stable.
- **The screen** is `steer.screen_draws`, unchanged: at least half the positive-control hosts must eat ≥ 1 item; the
  pool is extended once; with fewer than 36 admissible draws, the point fails its gate. It is run **by the planted
  command**, with the point's own G8(a) and G8(c) plants as the positive-control hosts, and writes `battery.json` and
  `reachability.json` beside the planted calls.

## 3. The planted set in the tree (B3), and the probe command

**`planters.py`** builds the plants. Its `planted POINT CONFIG OUT --hosts HOSTS_ROOT` command runs, per point:
1. **Hosts.** 8 designed and 8 holistic hosts come from RBT-113's committed O1 U-line finals (`ckpt/rbt-113-O1`, seeds
   1–3). They are walked in a fixed permutation (`default_rng([129, 132, fauna])`), and each is taken if it can carry
   its plant. Every refusal is logged.
2. **Tuning** on the point's first 4 pool draws, by intact − decoy F:
   - G8(b) over 16 variants;
   - G8(c) over its 2 signs;
   - G8(a) is signed by RBT-103's two direction probes, which must agree, as RBT-125 §B signs its hosts.
3. **The screen**, with the G8(a) and G8(c) plants as the positive controls; it writes the battery.
4. **The calls**, via `steer.call_genome`, at the point's τ:
   - (a), (b) and (c), 8 hosts each;
   - (d) and (e), 3 tumblers each;
   - the motors-off bodies (the 8 (a) plants with every Effector silenced).

   K3 and K4 are read as RBT-129 §5.5 registers them (`k3_k4`), and the power line is printed beside them.

**`probe_members.py RUN SEASON SEED RNG POINT OUT --battery B --config C`** is Stage P's probe command. Per fauna:

**Three launcher changes this needs, which are RBT-129's to make (not made here):**
- **(i) Order.** `probe_jobs` emits a point's probe lines *before* its planted line, but the planted command writes the
  battery the probes read. The planted line must come first. As it stands, `probe_members.py` refuses on the missing
  battery.
- **(ii) Pins.** The emitted script pins only `steer.py`'s blob. It should also pin `planters.py` and
  `probe_members.py`, and the RBT-97 and RBT-103 files `planters.py` imports.
- **(iii) Hosts.** `HOSTS_ROOT` is `ckpt/rbt-113-O1`, restored. `pays-steps` already restores it.

## 4. The holistic nose step (B3): a design, with an argued fallback

**The problem.** RBT-129 §5.1 registers holistic PAYS as "the G8(c) plant at a = 6 on 8 holistic hosts: its F with a
lower bound > 0, **and its nose step against a speed step** as above". `steps.py` (RBT-125 §B) refuses non-designed
hosts (S13), because its nose motif is the Pioneer's routed compass.

**The design (recommended): a holistic step harness, `steps.py`'s arms with G8(c) as the motif.** Per holistic host,
on the same 128 paired seeds as §B, in the cell's `config.json`:

| arm | what | parity with §B (R6) |
|---|---|---|
| `c0` | the host as it is | = §B's `w0` |
| `c3`, `c3.4` | G8(c) at w = 3 (a = 6), and every output link one weight σ (0.4) more: **the nose step** | = §B's `w3` → `w3.4`. The step is per link, as §B's is. G8(c) may have more output links than the compass's two, so its gain step is larger in total, and the count is printed per host |
| `speed@c0`, `speed@c3` | `world.joint_damping / 1.25`: every torque motor's free-spin speed is gear ÷ damping | = §B's `speed@w`. The **realised** CoM speed ratio r is measured, and a host with r < 1.10 leaves the per-unit comparison, as in §B |

- **The reading** is §B's own (`reading()`, t intervals): nose step − speed step, paired per host, raw and per realised
  unit of speed.
- **R4** (perception paid, no blind-motion premium): holistic PAYS needs the G8(c) F bound > 0, **and** the nose step
  not to fall behind the speed step (§B's COMPARABLE or NOSE).
- **R6** (parity): same seeds, same block, same step sizes, same damping step, same per-unit rescale, and same reading.
  The hosts differ by construction (8 holistic against §B's 15 designed), and the host count is stated.
- **Its known weakness:** damping ÷ 1.25 is a clean +25% only for wheel-like torque motors. RBT-125's adversary measured
  the realised ratio at 0.49–1.32 across bodies. The harness therefore reads the per-unit column, and reports the
  hosts that leave it. If more than half of a cell's hosts leave it, that cell's holistic nose step is **NOT READABLE**,
  and PAYS falls back as follows.
- **Cost:** 8 hosts × 7 arms × 128 seeds × 18 cells, about 129,000 seasons, about 13 core-h at 0.36 core-s.
- **Implementation:** after the ruling, as `holistic_steps.py`, reusing `steps.py`'s `reading()` and `t_int()` with
  `planters.plant_c`, and with fixture tests.

**The argued fallback: holistic PAYS = G8(c)'s F bound alone, with no speed comparison.** This is weaker. F > 0 shows
that smell information pays the plant, but R4's clause about a blind-motion premium is left unanswered. So it would be
written "holistic PAYS (information only; no speed comparison)" wherever it is used. I recommend it only for cells
where the harness is NOT READABLE.

## 5. Power at τ = 2 s for the probe calls (`probe_power.py` → `probe_power.txt`)

The confirmed-share priors are the caricature's (`design-adversary/tau_probe.txt`): two-nose 0.48 → 0.40 and one-nose
0.32 → 0.24, from τ = 1 s to τ = 2 s. They are priors; each point's K3 and K5 measure the real rates. Every planted
and probe output prints its point's line.

**A finding about RBT-129's registered rule, not about τ.** PERCEIVES' STEERS clause is "the pooled confirmed STEERS
count exceeds the upper 95% binomial bound of the count expected from the founders' own confirmed false-STEERS rate".
Read literally, with the founders' rate plugged in, this **fires on 22–25% of null points**. With few false STEERS the
founders' count is usually 0, the bound is then 0, and one evolved STEERS passes. Taking the founders' rate at its
exact (Clopper–Pearson) upper 95% bound holds the null at 0.00–0.01. PERCEIVES also needs f BH-significant with mean
f ≥ F_MIN, which limits the damage. **This is RBT-129's to rule on; RBT-132 does not change the rule.** Both readings
are printed.

**What the probe leg can and cannot conclude at τ = 2 s** (N = 80, the pilot's 4 seeds × 20; exact-bound reading):

| q (share truly steering) | two-nose, τ 1 → 2 s | one-nose, τ 1 → 2 s |
|---|---|---|
| 0.10 | 0.09 → 0.05 | 0.02 → 0.01 |
| 0.25 | 0.79 → 0.63 | 0.41 → 0.19 |
| 0.50 | 1.00 → 0.99 | 0.95 → 0.78 |

- **It can conclude PERCEIVES** where about half the members steer (either route), or a quarter steer through two
  noses.
- **It cannot read an absence as a clean negative** where steerers are rare, or one-nose steering below about half.
  There NONE means "not detected at this sensitivity", and the point's K3 says whether the instrument could see
  steering there at all. K3's false-VOID rate is 0.011 at τ 2 s, against 0.001 at τ 1 s.
- **RBT-116's 0.397 (`power_tau2.txt`) is a different quantity:** the line-crossing count at K + 2 over 24 units. The
  probes are pooled counts against the founders.
