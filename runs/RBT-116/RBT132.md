## 1. τ per point (B1)

- `REGISTERED_POINTS` gains RBT-129's 18 PAYS cells (`RBT129_POINTS`, in `blocks.PAYS_CELLS` order; the 4 pilot points
  are among them). Each row is the committed `runs/RBT-129/worlds/<id>.config.json` **as `SimConfig` reads it** (the raw
  JSON omits `smell_contrast` and `smell_tau` at L points and `clear_from` everywhere, so those are the code's
  defaults):
  - G points: `smell_contrast` 2.5, `smell_tau` 2.0;
  - L points: `smell_contrast` 0 (no channel), with `smell_tau` 2.0 sitting unused;
  - everywhere: `eat_from root`, `eat_rule surface`, `clear_from root`, `eat_radius` 0.35.
- **`assert_registered_channel(cfg, point)`** refuses a channel whose τ differs from **that point's** registered τ, a
  channel at a point that registers none (an L point), and any unregistered point (KeyError). W1's check is as before.
- **`run_season(..., point)` and `point_season(point)`** carry the point. `screen_draws(world=<an RBT-129 point>)` uses
  that point's season by default. The CLI (`--world`) and `_job` pass the point through.
- **`assert_fair_config(raw, point)`**: an RBT-129 point runs only on a config carrying RBT-128's `"fairness": "fair"`.
- **`assert_point_world(raw, point)`** (S1): the config's whole `sim` block must hash to the point's committed block, so
  a G config cannot run under another G point's label, pool and τ (the adversary found 144 of 306 cross-pairs passing
  the smell-and-eating check alone). It is checked in the CLI, `planted`, `pays` and `probe`.

## 4. Holistic PAYS and the holistic nose step (B3)

**Holistic PAYS' F leg** (`planters.py pays`, S7c): G8(c) at a = 6 on 8 holistic hosts **with two single-instance
noses** (the carrying share is printed per cell); the screen with those plants; each host's stage-2 F (mean over the
stage-2 draws); **holistic F = the mean over hosts of each host's stage-2 F, with a one-sided 95% t bound over hosts**
(the host is the replication unit). The F leg PAYS when that bound is > 0.

**The nose-step leg: a harness running `steps.py`'s arms with G8(c) as the motif** (implementation authorised only
after this fix-check passes). Per holistic host, on §B's 128 paired seeds, in the cell's `config.json`, **5 arms**:

| arm | what | parity with §B |
|---|---|---|
| `c0` | the host as it is | = §B's `w0` |
| `c6` | G8(c) at a total a = 6, split evenly over its n output links | = §B's `w3` (a = 6 over the compass's 2 links) |
| `c6.8` | the nose step: a total a = 6.8, split evenly (§B's step is +0.4 on each of 2 links, +0.8 in total) | = §B's `w3.4` |
| `speed@c0`, `speed@c6` | `world.joint_damping / 1.25` (every torque motor's free-spin speed is gear ÷ damping) | = §B's `speed@w`. The realised CoM speed ratio r is measured; a host with r < 1.10 leaves the per-unit comparison |

- **The reading** is §B's own (`reading()`, t intervals): nose step − speed step, paired per host, raw and per realised
  unit of speed.
- **RBT-121 R4** (a nose step pays at least comparably to a speed step): holistic PAYS needs the F leg **and** the nose
  step not behind the speed step (§B's COMPARABLE or NOSE).
- **RBT-121 R6** (operator parity): both faunas' hosts come from RBT-113 O1, at the default operators; the comparison is
  at the default operators, not at matched erosion. Measurement parity with §B is kept too: the same seeds, block, step
  size (as a total), damping step, per-unit rescale and reading. The hosts differ by construction (8 holistic against
  §B's 15 designed), and the count is stated.
- **Its known weakness:** damping ÷ 1.25 is a clean +25% only for wheel-like torque motors (RBT-125's adversary: realised
  0.49–1.32 across bodies). If more than half of a cell's hosts leave the per-unit column, that cell's holistic nose step
  is **NOT READABLE**.
- **Cost:** 8 hosts × 5 arms × 128 seeds × 18 cells = 92,160 seasons, about 9 core-h at 0.36 core-s.

**The fallback, only where the nose step is NOT READABLE: holistic PAYS = the F leg alone**, labelled "holistic PAYS
(information only; no speed comparison)". It shows that smell information pays the plant, but **it cannot exclude a
blind-motion premium, so it is barred from any §8 statement that needs R4.**

## 5. Power at τ = 2 s for the probe calls (`probe_power.py` → `probe_power.txt`)

**PERCEIVES' STEERS clause, as ruled (item 2):** a one-sided Fisher exact test at α = 0.05 of the pooled evolved
confirmed-STEERS count against the pooled founders' count, with the same members per seed. Its null, computed over the
founders' rate ε ∈ {0.001, …, 0.10}, is **≤ 0.028 at N = 80 and ≤ 0.033 at N = 160**. The old plug-in reading fired on
12–25% of null points; readings A, B and C are rejected. The f conditions (BH, mean f ≥ F_MIN) still gate the call, and
f and the call share their stage-2 draws, so they are a conjunction, not two independent tests (R8).

**K3's false-VOID under the ruled SEEN (item 1):** the caricature's SEEN share (`k3_seen_probe.txt`) is 1.00 for the
two-nose plant at τ 1 and 2 s, and 1.00 / 0.96 for the one-nose plant; K3's false-VOID is **0.000 at both τ**, under
0.05 (no report needed). A moderate one-nose steerer that never clears F_MIN (steer1 k 8) is SEEN at 0.72 / 0.88, where
the old K3 could never see it: the ruling's point. (These are caricature shares; the gate's K3 measures the real ones.)

**What the probe leg can and cannot conclude** (N = 80, the pilot's 4 seeds × 20, founders' ε 0.005):

| q (share truly steering) | two-nose, τ 1 → 2 s | one-nose, τ 1 → 2 s |
|---|---|---|
| 0.10 | 0.31 → 0.21 | 0.12 → 0.05 |
| 0.25 | 0.94 → 0.85 | 0.70 → 0.46 |
| 0.50 | 1.00 → 1.00 | 0.99 → 0.93 |

- **It can conclude PERCEIVES** where a quarter or more of the members steer through two noses, or about half through
  one nose.
- **It cannot read an absence as a clean negative** where steerers are rare (q about 0.1), or one-nose steering at a
  quarter. There NONE means "not detected at this sensitivity", and the point's K3 says whether the instrument could see
  steering there at all.
- **RBT-116's 0.397 (`power_tau2.txt`) is a different quantity:** the line-crossing count at K + 2 over 24 units.

Every planted, pays and probe output prints its point's line, computed (nothing is a literal).
