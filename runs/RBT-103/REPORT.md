### Its size depends on the food's patchiness (exploratory)

The adversary ran the cell I was missing — the ten new populations' own bodies in
P-801's world — and it supports the attribution more strongly than my one pair of moves
did (`runs/RBT-103/adversary/world_matrix.txt`):

- `t(9) own world          +0.844 [+0.618, +1.070]`
- `t(9) P-801 world        +2.267 [+1.461, +3.072]`
- `t(9) P-801 world - own  +1.423 [+0.823, +2.022]`
- `PAYS count, own world 8/10, P-801 world 4/10`

**All ten gain more in P-801's world, at 1.85× to 3.38× their home gain**, and W4b's
bodies read +2.219 there against +0.879/+1.007 at home. Bodies that never lived in that
world get the larger prize in it, so the size follows the world and not the lineage.

And it is **patchiness**, not density. Changing one food factor at a time on P-801's own
bodies:

| world (P-801's bodies) | a = 64 | base |
|---|---|---|
| P-801 world (26 items, 3 patches, regrow 45 s) | +3.018 | 2.77 |
| P-801 world, instant regrow | +3.502 | 3.24 |
| 12 items, 3 patches | +2.489 | 1.20 |
| 26 items, no patches | +1.308 | 2.77 |
| RBT-90 world (12, no patches, instant) | +0.940 | 1.27 |

**Density alone adds little and regrowth adds nothing.** It is not baseline scaling
either: the uniform 26-item world has the same base income as home and less than half the
gain. So the headline is **"its size depends on the food's patchiness" (exploratory)**;
"density" and "regrowing" are withdrawn.

Two residuals the adversary raised and I carry rather than resolve:

- **(a) Heterogeneity.** Seeds 807 and 2 barely rise in P-801's world.
- **(b) A home advantage is not excluded.** P-801's own bodies read +3.018/+3.926 at home
  against a foreign mean of +2.267, and only seeds 4 and 7 exceed them.

Seed-matched it is +1.007 → +0.949 on 7000+ and +0.879 → +1.094 on 9000+; the conclusion

one block and 3.4× on the other.

The positive controls reproduce their references **deterministically** — that is what
"to the digit" meant and it is not a statement about stability across draws.

**My prediction for this supplement was wrong, and the supplement is what shows it.**
I told the coordinator that 256 draws would "roughly halve" the retention interval.
It did not: the decoy's own interval goes from a mean width of 0.558 at 64 draws
to 0.506 at 256, a 9% narrowing rather than 50%. The reason is that this
interval is t(df = 6) **over seven bodies**, not over seeds; quadrupling the draws
shrinks only the within-body noise, and the between-body spread — which dominates —
is untouched by it. **Resolving the remaining three needs more bodies, not more
draws.** The compute estimate I gave was also low: 0.8 core-hours against the 1.6 the
run actually took.

**Seed 806 crosses to food-dependent** and the others stay just above the line, so
the four were sitting near the threshold rather than hiding a gait effect: **none reads
gait at either n.** It re-classes nothing in the primary table.

  any new population was read. The adversary confirms it is in the harness's first commit,
  before the first row, and that neither version could move a verdict since the check

- **The body-plan check reported every exception as "cannot carry the circuit"**, so a
