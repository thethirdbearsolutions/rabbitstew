runs/RBT-19/P-801, both direction probes in the tree.

at the config's own 15 s duration. So R = 0.506 on g500 is a property of the robot,
not of a probe that was too short, and the useful measurement is the SHAPE of the
distribution rather than a longer probe.

    within-bout R deciles:       0.42, 0.52, 0.64, 0.73, 0.78

  The reading. g500 drives FORWARD on 63/64 bouts under check and 64/64 bouts under td.
  It is not a robot with two directions, so its low R is not ambiguity BETWEEN
  bouts -- it is heading spread WITHIN a bout, which is what the low within-bout R
  deciles above say directly. Its sign is therefore not in doubt (the 1 bout(s) that read backward are the tail of a distribution centred well forward, not a second mode),
  and the fact that its anti-compass also pays is NOT explained by an uncertain
  direction. My report's speculation that 'neither sign is clearly its compass' is
  withdrawn: g500's compass is the one the arm installed, and why its inverse also
  pays on this robot is unexplained and stays on the record as unexplained.
