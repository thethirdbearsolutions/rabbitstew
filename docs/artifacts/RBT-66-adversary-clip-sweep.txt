A probe-tick with ONE side clipped is the dangerous case: the clip removes that
side's contribution, and since steering is (L+R)/2 and throttle is (L-R)/2, half the
surviving side's magnitude is moved onto the other axis. Both axes then read equal in
magnitude, so a one-sided clip cannot by itself pick a winner -- but it caps how far
either axis can exceed the other, which is exactly the clause the verdict rule tests.

|  | a probe-tick with BOTH sides clipped contributes exactly zero to the delta on both axes, whatever the circuit does. |
