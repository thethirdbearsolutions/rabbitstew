## Correction (RBT-97 adversary, qualification 3)
My report said the prize exists 'on fourteen of fourteen robots'. That is not in
RBT-67's data and it is withdrawn. What RBT-67 holds is TWELVE robots given their own
compass, all of which gain at every rung, and TWO given its inverse, both of which
lose at every rung. The two inverted ones were shown to gain with their own sign only
later: at a = 64 alone, in RBT-69's run (+3.734, +1.109) and again in this ticket's
own arm. The accurate sentence is: twelve of twelve gain across the ladder, and the
remaining two gain at the one rung where their own sign has been measured.
