# RBT-91 adversary: the routed motif's magnitude is a product of four weights and a slope

  a = (u_L - u_R)(v_L + v_R)/2 * sech^2(b); the install fixes u = +/-1 and reads a = 2w (RBT-87).
  Evolved copies have u, v and b free.  Everything below is the operator's own process or the
  committed weights; nothing is simulated.
