RABBITSTEW · ROBOTIC ARTIFICIAL BRAIN/BODY-INTERTWINED SIMULATION TOOLKIT AND EVOLUTION WORKSHOP

Evolving robot bodies and brains together

Rabbitstew is a physical robot simulator in which a robot's body and its neural controller are both written in its genome, so both can evolve. It began as a 2005 proposal to test, side by side, whether evolving body and brain together beats evolving a controller inside a fixed, human-designed body. This site holds the plain-language progress reports, the founding proposal and the research papers.
